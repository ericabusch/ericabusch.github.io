Junie's Pupdates from Korea

(only for the folks who are special enough to get this link - pls don't share with strangers)

14-24 August 2026

Our mission over the last 10 days was getting moved into our new, permanent apartment, fixing a million plumbing issues, and obtaining furniture. Junie attempted to be helpful by splashing in all the loose water she could and by blending into the scenery. She's kept herself busy befriending all the old ladies and small children in the neighborhood and being a Very Good Ambassador on behalf of All Large Dogs.

7-13 August 2026

It's been crazy hot and humid in Suwon, so we've been getting creative in figuring out how to keep Junie (and us) cool and active.

We decided to get out of the city for the weekend and find somewhere near the coast. The only hotel we could find which was "large dog friendly" was Little Mykonos -- a confusingly Mediterranean themed resort with a specific building for dogs, including a dog pool which we thought would be perfect for Junie. This was on Seonjaedo island, which has some crazy huge mud flats.

1-6 August 2026

Junie was a true champ on the long flights from NYC -> Seattle and Seattle -> Incheon/Seoul. She brought a favorite toy and her beloved blanket for some comfort on the flight. She had lots of passengers petting her as she flew and even got some treats and compliments from the flight attendants and pilot.
